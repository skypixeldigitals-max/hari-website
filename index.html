Frequently asked questions

When is HARI launching?expand_more

We're in final testing in Colombo. Join the list and you'll be the first to know the date — we'll email you once, on launch day.

Which areas will you cover first?expand_more

Colombo and the immediate suburbs at launch. If you're outside that area, tell us where you are and we'll use it to decide where we go next.

How do you verify your professionals?expand_more

Four gates, every time: a scan of both sides of the NIC, a selfie holding that NIC, a police report, and a manual review by a person at HARI who can reject the application.

How is the price decided?expand_more

A verified professional visits first and looks at the actual job, then gives you a fixed quote. You approve it before any work starts, and it can't creep up afterwards.

How do professionals get paid?expand_more

HARI holds the client's payment in escrow while the job is on. Once the work is confirmed done, it's released. The professional is never left chasing a customer for money.
