Privacy

This page describes what this website does with your information. Our full privacy policy, covering the HARI app, will be published here before launch.

What we collect

Only what you type into a form on this site: your email address, and — if you use the coverage form — the name of your town. If you sign up as a professional, we also record that you asked as a professional rather than as a customer.

What we do with it

We email you once, when HARI launches. Town names are used to decide which areas we open next. That is all.

Who else sees it

Form submissions are stored in Google Forms, which means Google processes them on our behalf. We do not sell your information and we do not share it with anyone else.

Removing your details

Message us on WhatsApp at +94 74 059 1853 and we will delete your entry.

Cookies

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or tracking. Your language choice is stored in your own browser and never leaves your device.
